How it works

  1. Post. A donor lists the food, quantity, pickup address and a collect-by time.
  2. Claim. An NGO sees open listings sorted by distance and deadline, and claims one.
  3. Collect. The NGO marks the food as picked up, then delivered.
  4. Count. Every delivered serving is added to the donor's and NGO's totals.
